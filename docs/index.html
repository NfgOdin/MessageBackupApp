🛡️ 100% On-Device Privacy Guaranteed

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Muninn: Message Memory & Vault ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data and personal information are treated when using the Muninn mobile application on Android.

Our Privacy Promise: We do not operate remote servers to collect, harvest, monitor, inspect, or sell your private text messages, photos, phone numbers, or contact records. Everything stays on your device.

1. On-Device Processing & Zero Data Transmission

  • Strictly Local: Message querying, contact resolution, Conversation Insights analytics, promise detection, and document formatting (PDF, HTML, Desktop Offline Viewer, CSV, Plain Text) occur exclusively on your local device.
  • No Cloud Servers: We maintain zero proprietary servers storing or transmitting your personal conversations or media.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: Exported archives remain in your private device storage or chosen folder until you explicitly choose to share them via Android's native system share sheet or connect your own cloud storage.

2. Artificial Intelligence (O.D.I.N. AI & Commitment Radar)

  • Local AI Mode (Ollama): When using an on-premise Ollama endpoint on your private network, 100% of your message data stays entirely within your private home network.
  • Cloud AI Mode (Google Gemini BYOK): When using cloud AI, queries are executed using your own personal Gemini API key directly to Google's official endpoints. Message excerpts are transmitted solely to fulfill your explicit query.
  • Commitment Radar: Uses on-device pattern recognition and regex. No message data is transmitted to third parties for radar detection.

3. Biometric & Vault Security

Muninn supports biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) and salted SHA-256 PIN protection. Biometric authentication is handled by Android's hardware-backed Keystore and BiometricPrompt system. Biometric data is never accessible to Muninn or transmitted off-device.

4. Permissions Used and Why

  • READ_SMS & RECEIVE_MMS: Required to read past conversation threads and embedded multimedia attachments for archiving.
  • READ_CONTACTS (Optional): Used only to match phone numbers with local address book names. Can be disabled at any time in Settings.
  • INTERNET: Required for Google AdMob advertising, Google Play In-App Billing, and user-initiated cloud backup syncs (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, WebDAV).
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Required to unlock the application and uncloak the private Media Vault.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to notify you of completed scheduled auto-backups and export status.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Billing Partners

Muninn integrates compliant third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Delivers advertising (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video, and Rewarded Interstitials) and handles regional GDPR/UK consent. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes one-time upgrades to remove ads, unlock lifetime Pro features, and accept voluntary tips securely.
