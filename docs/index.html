Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Message Backup & Export ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is treated when using our mobile application.

1. On-Device Processing & Zero Data Transmission

Your privacy is our core engineering principle:

  • Strictly Local: All message querying, contact resolution, document generation (PDF, HTML, Markdown), and zip compression occur entirely on your local device.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote servers to store, monitor, inspect, or sell your personal SMS/MMS messages, attachments, or contact records.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: Exported files remain in your local app cache or designated folder until you explicitly choose to share them via the standard Android system share sheet.

2. Permissions Used and Why

  • READ_SMS & RECEIVE_MMS: Required to read your conversation histories and extract media parts for formatting into documents.
  • READ_CONTACTS (Optional): Used only to resolve phone numbers into contact book names for easy identification. Can be disabled in Settings at any time.
  • INTERNET: Required exclusively by Google AdMob (to serve ads/video rewards) and Google Play Billing (to process in-app purchases and tips).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Billing Partners

The application integrates compliant third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Delivers advertising and handles GDPR/UK consent in Europe. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes one-time upgrades to remove ads and voluntary tips securely.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy statement, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or support channels.
