MUNINN: Message Understanding & Neural Intelligence Navigation Nexus

In ancient lore, the Allfather Odin dispatched his sacred raven Muninn across the realms to remember all words, oaths, dates, and truths. Today, Muninn is your private digital memory vault—safeguarding your SMS & MMS messages, photos, and conversations with 100% on-device privacy, court-ready legal exports, and intelligent neural search.

🛡️ ZERO DEVELOPER SERVERS — 100% LOCAL PRIVACY
Your private text messages, photos, and contacts belong to you alone. Muninn operates entirely on your device:
• No accounts required to back up or read messages
• Zero messages uploaded to developer servers
• Local database encrypted and stored on your hardware
• All exports and archives stay on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them

📄 MULTI-FORMAT DOCUMENT EXPORT
Transform your text messages into rich, beautifully formatted archives:
• 📑 Court & Legal PDF: Clean timestamped transcripts with embedded multimedia images, header evidence data, and page numbers.
• 🌐 Offline Interactive HTML: A self-contained, interactive chat viewer that looks and behaves like a modern messaging app—opens in any browser on PC, Mac, or Linux without installing software.
• 📊 Spreadsheet CSV & Excel: Searchable columns for date, time, sender, recipient, direction, and body text.
• 📝 Markdown & Plain Text: Clean, universal text formats for Obsidian, Notion, or simple text archiving.
• 🔒 Encrypted ZIP Archives: Secure your exports with industry-standard AES-256 password protection.

🧠 O.D.I.N. AI INTELLIGENCE & COPILOT
Meet O.D.I.N. (Omniscient Document & Insights Navigator)—your intelligent message archivist:
• ⚡ 1-Tap Google AI & On-Device Gemini Nano: Ask questions about your message history with zero setup or API keys.
• 🔑 Instant Information Extraction: Quickly retrieve forgotten gate codes, Wi-Fi passwords, addresses, flight numbers, and dates.
• 📅 Commitment & Promise Radar: Automatically scans message threads to detect upcoming deadlines, promises, and financial commitments.
• ⚖️ Grounded Truth: Answers are grounded directly in your actual message records with verifiable dates and timestamps.
• 🔒 Private & Local: Supports on-device hardware inference (AICore / Gemini Nano) and private local hosts (Ollama).

💬 RAVEN MESH — OFF-GRID WALKIE-TALKIE & P2P
Stay connected even when cellular networks fail:
• 🎙️ Push-to-Talk (PTT) Walkie-Talkie: Real-time high-fidelity voice streaming over Bluetooth LE, local Wi-Fi, and global relay.
• 📦 Raven Parcels: Securely beam encrypted images, audio recordings, and files between devices.
• 📴 100% Off-Grid Capable: Works without internet, cell towers, or satellite connections.
• 🔒 Loki’s Mist: Optional self-destructing, timed ephemeral messaging for maximum privacy.

📊 CONVERSATION INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS
Discover fascinating trends and hidden patterns in your messaging habits:
• Total message counts, incoming vs. outgoing ratios, and media attachment counts
• Top contact rankings and chat frequency over time
• Visual message activity charts and response rhythm heatmaps

⏰ SCHEDULED AUTOMATIC BACKUPS
Set it and forget it! Configure Muninn to automatically back up your conversations daily, weekly, or monthly to your device storage or private cloud folders.

🔐 BIOMETRIC & PIN VAULT LOCK
Keep your private conversations and exported archives safe from prying eyes with fingerprint, face unlock, or a custom Master Security PIN.
