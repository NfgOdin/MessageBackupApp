# Privacy Policy for Message Backup & Export

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

**Message Backup & Export** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Message Backup & Export mobile application on Android.

---

## 1. On-Device Processing & Zero Data Collection
- **100% On-Device:** All operations performed by Message Backup & Export—including querying your SMS/MMS messages, reading local contact names, formatting documents (PDF, HTML, Markdown, Plain Text), and packaging zip archives—occur exclusively on your local device.
- **No Cloud Transmission:** We do **not** operate any backend servers to collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal text messages, photos, phone numbers, or contact records.
- **User-Initiated Sharing:** When you choose to export your messages, files are created either directly in your chosen device folder or handed off to Android's native system Share Sheet. You maintain complete control over where your exported archives are sent (e.g. Google Drive, email, or local storage).

---

## 2. Permissions Used and Why
Message Backup & Export requests the following Android runtime permissions strictly for core functionality:
- `android.permission.READ_SMS` & `RECEIVE_MMS`: Required to read past conversation threads and embedded multimedia attachments so they can be archived into documents on your device.
- `android.permission.READ_CONTACTS`: Optional. Used solely to match phone numbers with local address book names so your exports and thread list display familiar contact names. You can disable this at any time in Settings.
- `android.permission.INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required by Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play In-App Billing to display ads, collect regional consent, and process purchases.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies:
- **Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP):** Used to display banner and rewarded video ads. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, consent is gathered via Google UMP prior to ad requests. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Play In-App Billing:** Used to process one-time in-app purchases ("Remove Ads Forever") and voluntary Tip Jar contributions. All payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
